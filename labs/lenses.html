In simple words

A converging (convex) lens brings parallel rays together at the principal focus F. The focal length f is the distance from the lens to F. A diverging (concave) lens spreads rays out.

To find an image, draw two of the three special rays from the top of the object. Where they meet is the top of the image. If they really meet, the image is real (it can go on a screen). If they only seem to come from a point, it is virtual.

Drag the object (green arrow) left and right, or use the slider.
