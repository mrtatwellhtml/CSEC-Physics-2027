In simple words

Everything is made of tiny particles that are always moving. This is the kinetic theory of matter. When you heat something, its particles move faster: temperature is a measure of their average kinetic energy.

In a solid they vibrate in fixed places. In a liquid they are still close but slide past each other. In a gas they are far apart and move fast in all directions.
