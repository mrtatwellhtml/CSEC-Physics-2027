In simple words

Heat always flows from hot to cold, in three ways.

Conduction: particles pass vibrations to their neighbours. Metals are best because their free electrons carry energy quickly. Convection: in liquids and gases, the hot part expands, becomes less dense and rises, and cooler fluid sinks to take its place. Radiation: infrared waves, which need no particles at all, so they even cross the vacuum of space.
