In simple words

A scalar has size only (mass, time, speed, energy). A vector has size and direction (force, velocity, displacement, acceleration, momentum).

When two forces act on one object, they combine into one resultant force. You can't just add the numbers, because the directions matter: 3 N and 4 N can give anything from 1 N to 7 N. Draw them to scale and measure.

Drag the tip of F₁ or F₂, or use the sliders. 1 square = 1 N.
