In simple words

Pressure is how concentrated a force is: P = F ÷ A (force ÷ area), measured in pascals (1 Pa = 1 N/m²). The same force on a small area gives a big pressure. That's why a drawing pin goes in easily and snowshoes stop you sinking.

In a liquid, the deeper you go the more liquid is above you, so the pressure is bigger: P = ρgh.

Common mistakes

Change areas into m² before you divide (1 cm² = 0.0001 m²). In P = ρgh, h is the depth below the surface, not the height from the bottom. Liquid pressure does not depend on the shape or width of the container.
