In simple words

Two graphs describe a journey. Learn the two golden rules:

Distance–time graph: the steepness (gradient) = speed. A flat line means the object is stopped. A steeper line means it is going faster.

Velocity–time graph: the gradient = acceleration and the area under the line = distance travelled. A flat line means steady speed.
