In simple words

Radioactive decay is random: you can't predict when one particular nucleus will decay. But with millions of nuclei, a fixed fraction decays in each second.

The half-life is the time for half of the radioactive nuclei to decay, or for the count rate to fall to half. After 1 half-life ½ is left, after 2 half-lives ¼, after 3 half-lives ⅛, and so on.
