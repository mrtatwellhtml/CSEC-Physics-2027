In simple words

Density tells you how much mass is packed into each cm³ of a material. ρ = m ÷ V (mass ÷ volume).

A block of wood and a block of iron can be the same size, but the iron has much more mass, so it is denser. Anything less dense than water (1.0 g/cm³) floats in water. Anything denser sinks.
