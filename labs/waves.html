In simple words

A wave carries energy from place to place, but the material it travels through does not travel with it. Watch the red particle: it only moves back and forth around one spot.

Transverse: particles vibrate at right angles to the direction the wave travels (light, water waves). Longitudinal: particles vibrate along the direction of travel, making compressions and rarefactions (sound).

Wave controls

Displacement–distance (a snapshot). Peak to peak = wavelength λ.

Displacement–time (the red particle). Peak to peak = period T.

Common mistake

The two graphs look the same. Always read the x-axis label first: distance on the axis means you can read λ, time on the axis means you can read T (then f = 1 ÷ T). Amplitude is measured from the middle line to a crest, not from a crest to a trough.
