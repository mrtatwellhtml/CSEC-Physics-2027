In simple words

Energy is never made or destroyed. It only changes from one form to another. This is the law of conservation of energy.

At the top of the ramp the skater has gravitational potential energy (GPE = mgh). Going down, it turns into kinetic energy (KE = ½mv²). With friction, some energy becomes thermal energy (heat), which is wasted, so the skater can't climb back as high.

Work, power and efficiency: a motor lifting a load

A motor lifts a load at a steady speed. Useful work done = force × distance = mgh. Power = work ÷ time. Efficiency = useful energy out ÷ energy in × 100%.
