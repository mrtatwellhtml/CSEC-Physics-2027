In simple words

A force can make things turn. Its turning effect is called its moment: moment = force × perpendicular distance from the pivot (unit: N m). A small force far from the pivot can balance a big force close to it. That's why a door handle is far from the hinges.

A beam balances when the total clockwise moment = total anticlockwise moment. This is the principle of moments.

Drag a weight along the beam, or use its slider. Drag it off the beam to remove it. The beam is marked in cm from the pivot.

Common mistake

Use the distance from the pivot, not from the end of the ruler. And use force in newtons: a 200 g mass has a weight of 0.2 kg × 10 N/kg = 2 N.
