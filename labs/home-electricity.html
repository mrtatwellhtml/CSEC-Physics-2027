In simple words

Power (in watts) is how fast an appliance uses energy: P = V × I. Electricity companies charge for energy in kilowatt-hours (kWh): energy = power in kW × time in hours. 1 kWh is a 1000 W appliance running for 1 hour.

A fuse is a thin wire that melts if the current is too big, cutting off the live wire. The earth wire protects you if a fault makes a metal case live.
