In simple words

A pendulum swings back and forth. One oscillation is one complete swing: out, back and returning to where it started. The period T is the time for one oscillation.

One swing is too quick to time accurately, so we time 20 oscillations and divide by 20. A longer string gives a longer period. If you plot T² against l you get a straight line, and its gradient tells you the strength of gravity, g.

Watch out: sources of error

Reaction time (about 0.2 s) when you start and stop the watch. Timing 20 oscillations shares this error over 20 swings, so it becomes small. Also count from zero as you start, use a small angle (under 10°), and measure l to the centre of the bob.
