In simple words

Sound is made by vibrations. It travels as a longitudinal wave, so it needs particles to pass the vibration along. It can't travel through a vacuum.

Pitch depends on frequency (more vibrations per second = higher note). Loudness depends on amplitude. An echo is a reflected sound. It travels to the wall and back, so distance = speed × time ÷ 2.

Human hearing: about 20 Hz to 20 000 Hz. Above 20 kHz is ultrasound, used for scans of unborn babies, cleaning jewellery, and sonar to find the depth of the sea or shoals of fish.
