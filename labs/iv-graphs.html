In simple words

To test a component, change the voltage across it and measure the current through it. Then plot current I (y-axis) against voltage V (x-axis).

A straight line through the origin means I ∝ V, so the resistance is constant. This is Ohm's law (an ohmic conductor, like a resistor at constant temperature). A lamp's graph curves because its filament gets hotter and its resistance rises. A diode lets current through in one direction only.
