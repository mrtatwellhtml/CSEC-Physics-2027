In simple words

Light is one member of a family of electromagnetic (EM) waves. They are all transverse, can travel through a vacuum, and all travel at the same speed in a vacuum: 3 × 10⁸ m/s.

They differ in wavelength and frequency. In order from longest wavelength: Radio, Microwave, Infrared, Visible, Ultraviolet, X-ray, Gamma ("Rabbits Meet In Very Unusual eXotic Gardens"). Higher frequency means more energy, and the radiation is usually more dangerous.
