In simple words

1st law: if the forces on an object are balanced (resultant force = 0), it stays still, or keeps moving at the same speed in a straight line.

2nd law: an unbalanced (resultant) force makes it accelerate: F = ma. Bigger force → bigger acceleration. Bigger mass → smaller acceleration.

3rd law: if A pushes B, then B pushes A back with an equal force in the opposite direction.

Common mistakes

In F = ma, F is the resultant force (applied force − friction), not just the push. Mass (kg) is not the same as weight (N): weight W = mg is a force. And 3rd-law pairs act on different objects, so they never cancel each other out.
