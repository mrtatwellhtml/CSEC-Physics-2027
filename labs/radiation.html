In simple words

An unstable nucleus gives out radiation to become more stable. Alpha (α) is a helium nucleus (2 protons + 2 neutrons): big, slow, strongly ionising, stopped by paper. Beta (β) is a fast electron from the nucleus, stopped by a few mm of aluminium. Gamma (γ) is an electromagnetic wave, only reduced by thick lead or concrete.
