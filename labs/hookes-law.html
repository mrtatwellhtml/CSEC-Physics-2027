In simple words

When you hang a load on a spring, it gets longer. The extension is how much longer it gets: new length − original length.

Double the load and the extension doubles. That is Hooke's law: F = kx. It works only up to the limit of proportionality. Pull harder than that and the spring stretches unevenly, and it may never go back to its original length.

Live readings

Common mistake

Putting the length into F = kx. Always subtract the original length first to get the extension.
