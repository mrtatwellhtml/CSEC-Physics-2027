Learn by doing

Interactive labs

Each lab lets you change things and see what happens. Every lab has a short "In simple words" explanation, live working ("Show the maths"), tasks that tick themselves when you complete them, and a quick check.

How to use a lab

  1. Read In simple words first.
  2. Play with the controls. Predict what will happen before you press a button.
  3. Work through Try this. The tasks tick themselves when you have done them.
  4. Do the Quick check, then go back to the lesson and do the practice questions.
