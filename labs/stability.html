In simple words

The centre of gravity (C.G.) is the point where the whole weight of an object seems to act.

When you tilt an object, imagine a line going straight down from its C.G. While that line stays inside the base, the weight pulls the object back upright. Once the line falls outside the base, the weight turns it over, and it topples. A wide base and a low C.G. make an object more stable.
