In simple words

A transformer has two coils on an iron core. Alternating current in the primary coil makes a changing magnetic field in the core, and this induces an alternating voltage in the secondary coil.

More turns on the secondary → bigger voltage (step-up). Fewer turns → smaller voltage (step-down). Vs ÷ Vp = Ns ÷ Np. It does not work with d.c., because a steady current makes no changing field.
