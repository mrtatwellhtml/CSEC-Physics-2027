In simple words

Move a magnet into or out of a coil and a voltage (e.m.f.) is made in the coil. If the circuit is complete, a current flows. This is electromagnetic induction. It only happens while something is changing: a magnet sitting still in a coil makes nothing.

Faraday: faster movement, a stronger magnet or more turns → a bigger e.m.f. Lenz: the induced current always opposes the change that caused it, so the coil pushes back against the magnet.
