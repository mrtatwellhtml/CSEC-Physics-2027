In simple words

Everything contains positive charges (protons, fixed in the nucleus) and negative charges (electrons, which can move). Only electrons move. An object that gains electrons becomes negative; one that loses electrons becomes positive.

Like charges repel. Unlike charges attract. A charged object can even attract a neutral one by pushing or pulling the electrons inside it (induction).

Common mistake

Saying "positive charges moved". In solids, only electrons move. A positive object has lost electrons; it has not gained protons.
