In simple words

When ice melts or water boils, you keep heating but the temperature stops rising. Where does the energy go? It is used to break the bonds between particles so they can change state. This hidden energy is called latent heat.

E = mL. L (specific latent heat) is the energy needed to change the state of 1 kg without changing its temperature. Boiling needs far more energy than melting, because the particles must be pulled completely apart.
