In simple words

Angles are always measured from the normal, the dashed line at 90° to the surface. Reflection: angle of incidence = angle of reflection (i = r).

Refraction: light changes speed when it goes into a new material, so it bends. Going into glass (slower) it bends towards the normal. Coming out (faster) it bends away. If it hits the inside of the glass at more than the critical angle, none gets out: total internal reflection.

Common mistakes

Measuring angles from the surface instead of from the normal. In n = sin i ÷ sin r, i is the angle in air and r is the angle in the glass. Find sin of each angle first, then divide.
