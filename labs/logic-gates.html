In simple words

A logic gate is an electronic switch that makes a decision. Every input and output is either 1 (on, high voltage) or 0 (off, low voltage).

AND: output 1 only if A and B are both 1. OR: output 1 if A or B (or both) is 1. NOT: flips the input. NAND = NOT AND, and NOR = NOT OR (the little circle means "NOT").
