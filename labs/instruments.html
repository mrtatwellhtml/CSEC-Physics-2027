In simple words

Different instruments measure to different precision (the smallest step they can show).

Metre rule: to 0.1 cm (1 mm). Vernier caliper: to 0.01 cm (0.1 mm). Micrometer screw gauge: to 0.01 mm. Pick the instrument that suits the size of the object: a metre rule for a desk, a vernier for the diameter of a test tube, a micrometer for the thickness of a wire.
