In simple words

Momentum = mass × velocity (p = mv, unit kg m/s). A heavy lorry and a fast bullet both have lots of momentum. Momentum has a direction: right is +, left is −.

When objects collide or push apart, the total momentum before = total momentum after (as long as no outside force acts). Kinetic energy is only kept in an elastic collision.

Impulse: why crumple zones and airbags save lives

A 1000 kg car going at 15 m/s stops in a crash. The change in momentum is the same however it stops. Making the stopping time longer makes the force smaller: F = Δp ÷ Δt.
